Small Business Loan FAQs & General Knowledge

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1: What is a business loan and how can I use it?

A business loan is a commercial credit facility provided to registered companies for working capital, buying inventory, equipment financing, or office expansion. Funds cannot be used for personal expenses or speculative trading.

Q2: What is the difference between revenue and turnover?

In financial terminology, annual turnover and gross annual revenue refer to the total dollar amount of sales generated by your business over 12 months before deducting expenses.

Q3: What are the minimum requirements to qualify for an SME business loan?

Your business must have at least 12 months of active operating history, minimum annual sales/turnover of $100,000, and a registered business structure (LLC, Sole Proprietorship, or Corporation).

Q4: How long does approval take?

Preliminary pre-qualification is instant via our voice bot or web portal. Full loan underwriting takes between 24 and 48 business hours after complete bank statements are submitted.

Q5: Is collateral mandatory for a commercial working capital loan?

No collateral is needed for unsecured working capital loans under $100,000 USD. For amounts from $100,000 to $500,000, asset collateral (equipment or property) is required.

Q6: Do I need collateral for an SME loan?

No physical collateral is required for unsecured business loans up to $100,000. Loans exceeding $100,000 require equipment or real estate collateral.
