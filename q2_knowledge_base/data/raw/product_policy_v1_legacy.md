## 1. Legacy Product Overview (Historical Reference)

Under the 2024 Credit Framework (v1.0), commercial borrowing parameters were configured as follows:

- **Minimum Business Turnover**: $80,000 per annum (Note: Increased to $100,000 in 2026 Policy v2.0).
- **Minimum Time in Business**: 6 months (Note: Increased to 12 months in 2026 Policy v2.0).
- **Maximum Unsecured Limit**: $75,000 (Note: Increased to $100,000 in 2026 Policy v2.0).
- **Origination Charge**: 2.5% of total borrowing principal.

## 2. Legacy FAQ Section (Near-Duplicate Content)

### Q: What is the minimum revenue required for a business loan?
In our 2024 policy framework, businesses required at least $80,000 in annual turnover. *Please note: Current 2026 policies now mandate $100,000 minimum revenue.*

### Q: Can a company operating for less than 1 year apply?
In 2024, 6 months of operating history was sufficient. Under current 2026 standards, a minimum of 12 months operating history is mandatory.
