## 2. Standard Objections & Grounded Responses

### Objection 1: "Your interest rate is too high."
- **Root Cause**: Customer comparing unsecured business loan rates to residential mortgages or personal savings loans.
- **Grounded Response**:
  "I completely understand your concern about interest rates. Unlike personal or mortgage loans, unsecured business loans carry higher risk for lenders because we do not ask for your real estate as collateral up front. Our rates start at 9.5% APR based on your financial strength, and having prompt repayment helps qualify your business for lower rates on future working capital drawdowns."

### Objection 2: "There is too much documentation required."
- **Root Cause**: Friction in providing bank statements and tax filings.
- **Grounded Response**:
  "I hear you—gathering documents takes time! However, because we only require 6 months of bank statements and your business tax return, we can process preliminary approval within 24 to 48 hours without requiring property appraisals or lengthy physical audits."

### Objection 3: "I need approval today / immediately."
- **Root Cause**: Urgent cashflow crunch or supplier payment deadline.
- **Grounded Response**:
  "We understand speed is critical for your business. While full underwriting takes 24 hours, if you complete your digital document upload right now, our automated risk engine can issue a soft preliminary conditional offer within 2 hours."

### Objection 4: "The maximum loan amount is insufficient for my expansion."
- **Root Cause**: Requesting >$100,000 without offering asset collateral.
- **Grounded Response**:
  "Our unsecured business loan option caps at $100,000. If your business requires up to $500,000, we can transition your application to our Secured Commercial Line, which utilizes equipment, invoices, or commercial assets to unlock larger capital pools."

### Objection 5: "The repayment period / tenor is too short."
- **Root Cause**: Monthly EMI payments are too high for short 12-month tenure.
- **Grounded Response**:
  "We offer flexible tenors ranging from 6 months up to 36 months for unsecured loans, and up to 60 months for asset-backed loans. Extending your tenor lowers your monthly installment, though it increases total interest paid over time."

### Objection 6: "I do not have collateral."
- **Root Cause**: Fear that real estate or equipment pledge is compulsory.
- **Grounded Response**:
  "Good news! For loans up to $100,000, no physical collateral or property pledge is required. We only require a standard personal guarantee from business owners holding 20% or more equity."

### Objection 7: "I do not want to share my bank statements or tax documents."
- **Root Cause**: Privacy concerns or hesitation to share sensitive financial data.
- **Grounded Response**:
  "We take data privacy very seriously. All financial documents are encrypted using 256-bit SSL protocols and are evaluated strictly by licensed risk algorithms for income verification. Without bank statements, we cannot assess monthly cashflow to approve your loan."
