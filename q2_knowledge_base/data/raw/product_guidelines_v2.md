## 1. Product Overview

Our Business Loan program provides fast, flexible financing to small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) for growth, working capital, and operational expansion.

### Loan Parameters Table
| Parameter | Unsecured Business Loan | Secured Equipment / Property Loan |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Loan Amount Range** | $10,000 – $100,000 | $100,001 – $500,000 |
| **Tenure / Duration** | 6 months to 36 months | 12 months to 60 months |
| **Annual Interest Rate (APR)** | 9.5% – 17.5% (Fixed APR) | 7.5% – 14.0% (Variable/Fixed) |
| **Processing Fee** | 2.0% of loan principal | 1.5% of loan principal |
| **Collateral Requirement** | None (Personal Guarantee required) | Hard asset (Equipment, Commercial Real Estate, Invoice Assignment) |
| **Repayment Frequency** | Monthly Equal Installment (EMI) | Monthly Equal Installment (EMI) |

## 2. Permitted & Unsupported Loan Purposes

### Permitted Uses of Funds:
- Working capital replenishment (inventory, payroll, supplier payouts)
- Equipment purchase, facility upgrades, business expansion
- Refinancing existing high-cost commercial debt

### Strictly Unsupported Uses (Instant Rejection):
- Personal residential mortgage payments or personal leisure travel
- Gambling, speculative cryptocurrency purchases, or stock trading
- Illegal activities or un-registered business activities

## 3. Business Eligibility & Qualification Rules

To qualify for a Business Loan, the applicant enterprise must fulfill the following mandatory criteria:

1. **Operating History**: Minimum of **12 months** continuous active operation (24+ months preferred for premier interest rates).
2. **Annual Turnover / Revenue**: Minimum gross annual revenue of **$100,000 USD** (or local currency equivalent).
3. **Monthly Cash Flow**: Positive net cash balance in business bank accounts for at least 3 of the last 4 consecutive months.
4. **Business Registration**: Legally registered entity (LLC, Corporation, Sole Proprietorship, Partnership) with active business license.
5. **Applicant Age**: Primary business owner / applicant must be between **21 and 65 years of age**.
6. **Required Documentation**:
   - 6 consecutive months of official corporate bank statements.
   - Last 2 years of filed business tax returns (or audited P&L statement).
   - Valid government ID of primary business owners holding ≥20% equity.
   - Proof of business ownership and legal address.

## 4. Repayment Structure & Fees

- Repayments are structured as Equal Monthly Installments (EMI) debited automatically via direct debit or ACH on the 1st or 15th of each month.
- **Early Repayment / Prepayment**: Zero prepayment penalty after 6 months of prompt payments.
- **Late Payment Fee**: A grace period of 5 calendar days is allowed. Thereafter, a late penalty fee of $50 plus 1.5% monthly penalty interest applies on the overdue balance.
